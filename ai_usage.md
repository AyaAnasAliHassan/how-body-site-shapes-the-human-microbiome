# AI Usage

Project: How Body Site Shapes the Human Microbiome: Diversity and Composition Across Gut, Tongue, Skin, and Palm
Authors: Reel Elsadig & Aya Hassan (BIOL 5340, Module 12)

## How AI was used

- **Brainstorming:** AI tools were used to help brainstorm candidate project topics and narrow them down to a question that fits 16S marker-gene analysis.
- **Outlining:** AI helped outline the project proposal and the structure of this report (sections, pipeline order, and deliverables).
- **Writing support:** AI helped polish and sharpen the wording of the proposal and report drafts.

## What AI was NOT used for

- AI did not generate any data, results, figures, or statistics for this project.
- All analyses are run by the authors in QIIME 2 and Python (scikit-learn) on the public QIIME 2 Moving Pictures tutorial dataset.

## Verification

- Every AI-assisted passage was read, edited, and verified by both authors before inclusion.
- Technical claims (pipeline steps, tools, dataset details) were checked against the QIIME 2 documentation (docs.qiime2.org) and course materials.
- Any statement the authors could not verify was removed rather than kept.
